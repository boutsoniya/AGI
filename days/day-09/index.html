100 DAYS OF AGI · DAY 09 · 12 OCT 2026

Can AI learn continuously?

Humans update their knowledge without rebuilding the brain from scratch. AI systems still struggle with that.

learningadaptation
THE IDEA

Continual learning asks how a system can acquire new information over time while preserving useful old capabilities. A central challenge is catastrophic forgetting: learning something new can interfere with what was learned before.

WHY IT MATTERS

A system operating in the real world cannot assume that its environment stays frozen. New tools, users, facts and tasks appear continuously.

TRY THIS

Train a tiny classifier on task A. Then train it on task B without revisiting A. Measure both before and after. Next, add a small replay buffer or regularisation strategy and compare the trade-off.

Question → test → observe → compare → explain
WHERE IT BREAKS

Keeping everything forever is expensive, while adapting too aggressively can overwrite useful knowledge.

TAKEAWAY

Learning over time is a stability–plasticity problem.
