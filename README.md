This repository is a public learning journal about **Artificial Intelligence, intelligence and AGI**.

It is intentionally **not a fixed syllabus or roadmap**. Topics can jump wherever the most interesting question leads — reasoning, memory, agents, multimodal AI, world models, robotics, evaluation, learning, research breakthroughs, failures and new ideas.

## 🌐 Open the journal

**[→ Open ONE IDEA / DAY](./index.html)**

Every daily drop contains:

- 🧠 a concept explained without unnecessary jargon
- ❓ a question to think about before reading
- 🔬 a small experiment or activity
- ⚠️ where the idea breaks
- 💡 a takeaway
- 📚 research and resources for going deeper

## Why this exists

AI is moving too quickly to understand through headlines alone.

Instead of trying to cover everything at once, this journal asks **one good question at a time**.

A day might be about:

> Can AI actually reason?

or:

> What does it mean for an AI to remember?

or:

> Can a world model help an agent plan?

or:

> What would count as evidence for AGI?

The next question is allowed to be completely different.

## 📝 Personal notebook

The journal also has a lightweight local notebook so you can save your own:

- observations
- disagreements
- questions
- experiment results
- ideas worth investigating

No account is required; notes are stored locally in the browser.

## The principle

This isn't about pretending we have solved AGI.

It is about becoming better at understanding **what intelligence might require, what today's systems can actually do, and where they still fail.**

**One idea. One experiment. One better question.**
