Does vision give AI understanding?

Seeing pixels is easy to describe. Understanding what those pixels mean for a task is much harder.

visionmultimodal
THE IDEA

Computer vision has moved from recognising objects toward richer representations: relationships between objects, actions, spatial structure and language. Multimodal models can connect visual inputs with textual concepts, but that does not automatically give grounded understanding.

WHY IT MATTERS

An intelligent system often needs to know not only what is visible, but what can be done, what changed, what is hidden, and what will happen next.

TRY THIS

Show an AI two nearly identical images with one object moved. Ask what changed, where it moved, and what action would be possible because of the change. Score each answer separately.

Question → test → observe → compare → explain
WHERE IT BREAKS

A model may describe an image fluently while missing spatial relationships or physical consequences.

TAKEAWAY

Perception becomes intelligence when it supports useful predictions and actions.
