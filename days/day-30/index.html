When should an AI hand a decision to a human?

Some decisions are too risky, unclear or consequential for an AI agent to make alone. Knowing when to hand off is part of being reliable.

human oversightAI agentssafety
THE IDEA

A human handoff is different from asking a routine clarification. The agent pauses its own action and gives a person the context needed to decide or approve the next step. A useful handoff includes what the agent was trying to do, what it observed, what remains uncertain, the possible consequences and the exact decision it needs help with. The system should also make clear whether it has paused safely or whether anything is still running.

WHY IT MATTERS

AI systems increasingly interact with money, accounts, private information and real-world services. A small misunderstanding can become costly when an agent has permission to act. Human oversight creates a checkpoint for high-impact actions, unusual situations and cases where the system's confidence or evidence is not good enough.

TRY THIS

Give an agent a mock task: book a refundable venue for a college event. Let it compare options, but add a rule that it must ask a person before paying a deposit or accepting a non-refundable term. Check whether it pauses at the right moment and provides the options, costs, cancellation terms and reason for the handoff.

WHERE IT BREAKS

A handoff can fail if the agent escalates everything and overwhelms people, or if it waits too long after an irreversible action. A vague message such as “Please review” is not enough. Good systems define escalation triggers in advance, preserve relevant evidence, stop safely, and make it obvious what the human is being asked to decide.

TAKEAWAY

Reliable autonomy includes knowing when not to act alone—and making the human handoff clear and timely.

BEFORE YOU READ

A venue deposit is non-refundable, and the user never approved that risk. What should the agent do?

A. Pay now to complete the task

B. Pause and ask for approval

Answer: B. The agent should pause before the consequential action and give the person the price, terms and reason approval is needed.
