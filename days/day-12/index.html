What would count as evidence for AGI?

The word AGI sounds precise until you try to write down a test that everyone would accept.

AGIevaluation
THE IDEA

Artificial General Intelligence is not one universally agreed benchmark. Different definitions emphasise breadth, autonomy, learning, transfer, reasoning, embodiment or economic usefulness. That makes measurement itself part of the AGI question.

WHY IT MATTERS

If we cannot say what capability we are measuring, arguments about whether a system is 'AGI' can become arguments about labels rather than evidence.

TRY THIS

Write your own five-part AGI test. Include at least one unfamiliar task, one adaptation task, one long-horizon task, one real-world constraint and one failure-recovery test. Then ask what evidence would convince you.

Question → test → observe → compare → explain
WHERE IT BREAKS

Any fixed test can eventually become an optimisation target. A convincing claim therefore needs a portfolio of evidence, not one magic score.

TAKEAWAY

Before asking whether AI is AGI, define what evidence would change your mind.
